# Prepared REDCap Shared Library Responses

## Instrument name

HEARTLAND Risk Assessment, version 1.0.1

## Abbreviation

HEARTLAND Risk Assessment

## Author

Vicky Muller Ferreira, MD; ORCID 0009-0009-1099-5690

## Contact

vickymuller@heartlandprotocol.org

## Description

Standalone research instrument containing the ten published HEARTLAND risk domains for rural and resource-limited heart-failure implementation studies: age, recent heart-failure hospitalization, renal function, natriuretic peptide, admission systolic blood pressure, diabetes, left ventricular ejection fraction, cardiovascular-kidney-metabolic stage, distance to cardiology, and limited social support. The package includes scored and unscored REDCap variants.

## Intended population and setting

Adults with heart failure enrolled in prospective research, feasibility, or implementation studies, particularly in rural and resource-limited U.S. settings.

## Scoring

The scored variant calculates a 0-18 weighted research score and categorizes it as low (0-4), moderate (5-8), or high (9-18). The unscored variant contains only raw inputs. The score has not been externally or prospectively validated and is not for clinical decision-making.
